[Intention-Setting with Travel]

[SHANNON]
I am travelling like crazy, sometimes 50 outings in a sit: brief but mundane travels, often using my astral hands to do things.
I keep intending to meet the Medicine Buddha but it has not happened.

[DANIEL]
All these things take patience and repetition.
You are only ten days in.
Set intentions once at the beginning of the sit (not during), and then let it go.
Paying attention makes it all happen.

[Visions]

[SHANNON]
I had several visions of a Jesus-like being outlined in dazzling light.
I was unable to stay with it long enough to ask his name.

[DANIEL]
Magick plays to our earliest childhood ethos and to cultural resonances.
